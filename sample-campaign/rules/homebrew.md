# Homebrew Rules — The Drowned Bell

- **Cursed Bell**: a creature that touches the Drowned Bell must make a DC 13 WIS
  save or gain 1 level of Grief each round it remains in contact.
- **Grief (homebrew condition)**: -2 to all attack rolls and ability checks that
  involve the creature's faith; the creature can't take reactions to damage from
  allies.
- **Wet & Cold**: in the sunken chapel, all ranged attacks have disadvantage; spell
  slots used underwater cost an extra action to recover.

These are homebrew and are NOT in any standard rulebook.
