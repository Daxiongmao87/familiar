# The Drowned Bell — Campaign Overview

Setting: a rain-soaked coastal city, Velmara, after the great bell-tower collapsed
and swallowed the lower ward. The party was hired to find out what fell from the
tower with it.

Running threads:
- The Guild wants the "Drowned Bell" recovered, but whispers say the bell is cursed.
- Brother Alric, a bell-keeper, died the night of the collapse; his body was never
  recovered.
- Mira's order wants the chapel below the tower sealed before it drowns entirely.

Party: Kael (rogue), Mira (cleric of the Drowned).
