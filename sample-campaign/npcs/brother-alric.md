# Brother Alric (NPC — Bell-Keeper, deceased)

Alric kept the Drowned Bell. He died the night of the tower collapse. His body
lies in the sunken chapel, slumped against the altar.

Secrets:
- Alric was smuggling the bell's resonance stones out of the guild's vault.
- He wrote a confession; the last page is in a waxed pouch sewn into his coat lining.
- The bell's curse is real: it turns the keeper's devotion to grief.

The confession pouch is the most valuable "loot" here.
