# The Sunken Chapel

A flooded chapel beneath the collapsed bell tower. Waist-deep black water. The
altar is dry. A bell-keeper's corpse (Brother Alric) is slumped against it.

Features:
- Brother Alric's body: a waxed confession pouch is sewn into the coat lining
  (DC 12 Sleight of Hand to find, DC 8 to cut free).
- A loose flagstone by the altar hides a resonance stone (DC 15 Investigation to spot).
- A trap: the altar floor is a pressure plate; stepping on it (DC 14 Perception to
  notice) drops the body into deeper water.
