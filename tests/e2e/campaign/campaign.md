# Vellmarsh — Campaign Overview

The party has arrived in Vellmarsh, a drowned market town. Beneath the chapel of
St. Aldric lies the **Sunken Crypt**, a flooded burial vault. The party has been
asked to investigate the missing grave-keeper, Malrik, and to recover the crypt's
lost ledger for the guild.
