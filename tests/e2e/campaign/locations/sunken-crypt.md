# The Sunken Crypt

The Sunken Crypt is a flooded burial vault beneath the chapel of St. Aldric.
Water is ankle-deep in the main chamber. Three marble sarcophagi stand against
the walls. A goblin corpse lies slumped beside the **north sarcophagus**.

## Features
- **Sealed door (east wall):** iron-bound, locked. Requires lockpicking.
- **North sarcophagus:** has a hidden false panel.
- **Goblin corpse:** near the north sarcophagus; carries a small pouch.

## Notes
The crypt smells of wet stone and old incense. A faint glow rises from the
water near the altar.
