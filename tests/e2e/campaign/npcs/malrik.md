# Malrik, the Grave-Keeper

Malrik is a gaunt, grey-haired man in a black shroud who has tended the Sunken
Crypt for thirty years. He is reclusive and distrustful of outsiders.

## Disposition
- Will trade information for a favor or a coin.
- Guards the **crypt ledger** (a bound book of burials and debts).
- Knows the location of every hidden cache in the crypt.

## What he knows
- The false panel in the north sarcophagus holds a **bone key**.
- The ledger lists a debt owed by the guild (120 gp).
